ConvergeSPH

The OHSU-PSU School of Public Health's Academic Operations Platform. ConvergeSPH currently manages course scheduling and offerings across both institutions, plus a faculty directory with tiered access. Below is what's planned next.

Faculty Operations

  • Faculty Documents — Categorized document references (HR, promotion, wages, reviews) linked directly to their SharePoint location. Access follows existing SharePoint permissions.
  • OHSU Annual Review — End-to-end tracking of the annual review process: faculty submissions, dossier and CV, Dean feedback, supervisor meetings and sign-off, and final signatures, visible on the faculty profile as it happens.
  • Adjunct Tracking — Automatic flagging when PSU adjuncts cross credit-hour thresholds for contract eligibility or administrative review, tracked over their respective rolling periods. Flags filterable across the faculty directory.
  • Committee Assignment & Tracking — Rules-based staffing for SPH committees, each with configurable membership requirements. Surfaces expiring terms, unplaced new faculty, member requests, and understaffed committees.

Curriculum

  • Course Objectives — Objectives and syllabi added directly to course detail pages, with future mapping of specific objectives to specific assessments.
  • Course History — A running record of curricular changes over time, including in-progress changes and access to their submission documents.

Programs & Competencies

  • Program Module — Introduces Programs as a structure above courses, with courses tagged as required, selective, or elective.
  • CEPH Competencies — Filter and map courses (and specific assessments) to CEPH accreditation competencies, at both the program and course level.
  • SPH Competencies — The same competency-mapping structure, built for SPH-specific learning objectives.
